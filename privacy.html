Privacy & Data Use

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to manufacturing companies, factories and related business entities that use this page in connection with their websites, social media accounts, advertisements, quotation requests and lead generation forms. The relevant business or factory is the company identified in the advertisement, social media profile, website or lead form through which you submitted your information (referred to in this policy as "we", "us" or "our").

2. Information We Collect

When you request a quotation, submit a lead form, contact us or otherwise communicate with the relevant factory or business, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and company name;
  • Phone number, WhatsApp number and email address;
  • Country or region;
  • Product requirements, application or intended use;
  • Estimated order quantity, dimensions, specifications, materials, artwork or customization requirements;
  • Shipping, delivery or project requirements;
  • Any information you voluntarily provide in messages, forms or quotation requests;
  • Basic lead source information, such as the advertising platform, form, campaign or advertisement that generated the inquiry.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Respond to inquiries and prepare quotations;
  • Discuss product specifications, customization, samples, production and delivery;
  • Contact you by phone, WhatsApp, email or another method you provided;
  • Manage customer relationships and follow up on business opportunities;
  • Improve products, customer service, lead forms and marketing performance;
  • Maintain business records and comply with applicable legal, accounting or regulatory obligations.

4. Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Other Lead Forms

If you submit information through Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or another advertising or social media platform, the information you choose to provide may be transmitted by that platform to the relevant factory or business. We use that information mainly to respond to your inquiry and provide product or quotation information.

Submitting a lead form does not create a binding purchase order, sales contract or obligation to buy. Final pricing and order terms depend on confirmed specifications, quantity, materials, customization, delivery terms and other project details.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with service providers or business partners that support customer management, communications, cloud storage, advertising, logistics, payment, production or other operational needs. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Inquiry and customer information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for quotation follow-up, customer service, business records, dispute handling and legal or accounting requirements. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to certain processing. You may also ask the relevant factory or business to stop contacting you at any time.

9. International Business and Data Transfers

Manufacturing businesses often work with customers in multiple countries. Information you submit may therefore be processed or stored outside your country or region. Where applicable, reasonable steps will be taken to handle such information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable legal requirements.

10. Third-Party Platforms and Services

Advertisements, websites and communications may use third-party platforms or services such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, email providers, CRM systems, cloud storage services or logistics providers. Their own handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

12. Contact

If you have a privacy question, want to update or delete information you previously submitted, or want us to stop contacting you, please contact the factory or business identified in the advertisement, social media profile, website or lead form through the official contact details provided there.
