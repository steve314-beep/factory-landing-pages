2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quotation, submit a Facebook or TikTok lead form, or otherwise communicate with us, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and company name;
  • Phone number, WhatsApp number and email address;
  • Country or region;
  • Product, packaging and order requirements;

  • Information you voluntarily include in messages, forms or quotation requests;

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and prepare quotations;

  • Improve our products, customer service, lead forms and marketing performance;
  • Maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Information From Facebook, Instagram and TikTok Lead Forms

If you submit information through a lead form on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or another advertising platform, the information you choose to provide may be sent to us by that platform. We use it for the same purposes described in this Privacy Policy, primarily to respond to your packaging inquiry and provide a quotation.

Submitting a lead form does not create a binding purchase order or contract. Final pricing and order terms depend on confirmed specifications, quantity, materials, printing requirements, delivery terms and other project details.

We do not sell personal information. We retain inquiry and customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for quotation follow-up, customer service, business records, dispute handling and legal or accounting requirements. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized.

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which can include requesting access, correction or deletion, or asking us to stop certain communications. You may also tell us at any time that you no longer wish to receive follow-up messages.
